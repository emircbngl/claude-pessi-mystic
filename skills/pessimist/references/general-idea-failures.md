# General idea / plan / decision failures

Use this **instead of** the software clusters when the target isn't code — a business idea, a strategy, a life/career decision, a plan, a bet. (Assumptions and tail risks still apply on top.) The discipline is the same: comprehend it, then enumerate every way it ends badly, followed to the realistic worst case. Ask: **"if this goes wrong, how exactly, and how bad does it get?"**

## Premise & framing
- What's the core belief this rests on, and what if it's wrong? (See `assumption-failures.md`.)
- Is this solving a real problem, or one that sounds important? Is the problem even the bottleneck?
- Is the goal clear and measurable, or vague enough that you can't tell success from failure?
- Are you choosing this because it's right, or because it's available/familiar/exciting/least-scary?

## Resources & runway
- What does this consume — money, time, attention, credibility, relationships, energy — and what if it costs 2–3× that?
- What runs out first, and what happens at that moment? Can you sustain it long enough to reach the payoff?
- What else can't happen because this is eating the resources (the opportunity cost — see below)?
- What if the return takes far longer to arrive than planned, or never arrives?

## Reversibility & lock-in
- If this goes wrong, how hard is it to undo? Is it a one-way door or a two-way door?
- What does it commit you to — contracts, sunk cost, a public position, a path you can't easily exit?
- Does it foreclose other options (burned bridges, a narrowed identity, a reputation that boxes you in)?
- How long until you'd even *know* it's not working — and how much is lost in that lag?

## People & incentives
- Who has to cooperate, approve, fund, or not-resist for this to work? What if they don't?
- Whose interests does this threaten, and how will they push back?
- Are the incentives aligned, or is someone rewarded for it failing / for the status quo?
- Who's relying on this, and what's the damage to them if it falls through?

## Timing & environment
- Why now? What if the timing is wrong — too early (no one ready), too late (window closed)?
- What external condition does this depend on (market, season, a trend, someone else's move) that could shift?
- What changes in circumstances — yours or the world's — would invalidate the whole thing midway?

## Opportunity cost & the slow failure
- What's the *best alternative* use of these resources, and is this clearly better — or just the one in front of you?
- What's the failure that isn't dramatic: it doesn't blow up, it just slowly doesn't work, drains you, and you stay in it too long because quitting feels like losing?
- What's the sunk-cost trap — the point where you'll keep going *because* of what you've already spent, not because it's still right?

## Second-order & human consequences
- And then what? Chain the consequences past the obvious first effect (see `worst-case-projection.md`).
- What's the effect on the people around it — strain, resentment, damaged trust, harm — that the plan ignores?
- What's the worst realistic outcome, named plainly: what exactly is lost, who is hurt, and is it recoverable?
