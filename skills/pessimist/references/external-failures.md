# External failures

The forces you don't control and can't fix — law, markets, platforms, money, the world. You can build everything right and still be killed from outside. Ask: **"what change out in the world, that we have no power over, ends this?"**

## Legal, regulatory & compliance
- What law/regulation applies (privacy/GDPR/CCPA, accessibility, financial, health/HIPAA, export, age/COPPA, sector rules) — and what if you're not compliant?
- What if the rules change, or are newly enforced, after you've built around the old ones?
- Are there licensing, IP, patent, or trademark exposures — using something you don't have the right to, or a name someone owns?
- What contractual obligations (SLAs, DPAs, terms) could you breach, and what's the penalty?
- Is there liability if the thing fails, gives wrong output, or is misused — and who's holding it?

## Platform & ecosystem dependence
- Whose platform do you live on (app store, cloud, social API, browser, OS, marketplace)? What if they change the rules?
- What if they deprecate an API you depend on, change pricing, tighten review, or ban your category?
- What if the platform ships your feature natively, or decides your app competes with them?
- What if an algorithm/policy change cuts off your distribution or discovery overnight?

## Market & economic
- What macro shift hurts you — recession, rate changes, a funding winter, customers cutting budgets?
- What if a competitor undercuts, out-markets, or out-raises you? What if an incumbent wakes up?
- What if customer demand shifts, the trend you're riding fades, or the hype cycle turns?
- What if the cost structure (compute, API, labor, energy) rises and breaks your unit economics?

## Funding & runway
- What if the money runs out before this reaches sustainability? What's the runway, and what's the burn?
- What if a key customer/contract/grant/investor walks? How concentrated is the revenue?
- Does the whole thing depend on continued funding/sponsorship/free-tier generosity that can be withdrawn?

## Reputation & trust
- What's the headline-risk scenario — the breach, the outage, the offensive output, the viral complaint?
- What if early users have a bad experience and the reputation never recovers (you get one first impression)?
- What if the project gets associated with something toxic (a bad actor using it, a PR incident, a founder scandal)?
- Trust is asymmetric: years to build, one incident to lose. What's the one incident here?

## Force majeure & the wider world
- What if a key person, vendor, partner, or supplier simply collapses or disappears?
- Geopolitical/infrastructure: sanctions, outages, region lockouts, a major cloud provider's bad day taking you with it?
- What's the "we never imagined that would happen" event that's actually happened to others in this space before?
