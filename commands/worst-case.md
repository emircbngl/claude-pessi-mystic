Read the `pessimist` skill and `references/worst-case-projection.md`, then project the worst case.

Decision/plan: $ARGUMENTS
(If empty, ask which decision to project.)

1. **Comprehend the decision** (`references/comprehension.md`): what's being chosen, what success looks like, and crucially how reversible it is.
2. **Find the failure points** — sweep the taxonomy (`references/failure-taxonomy.md`) enough to identify where this decision can go wrong, with emphasis on the load-bearing assumptions (`references/assumption-failures.md`).
3. **Chain each one forward** with "and then what?" to its worst *realistic* end-state — second/third-order effects, correlated failures, worst-possible timing, and the human reaction that makes it worse.
4. **Name the cost at the bottom** of each chain: what's lost, who's hurt, whether it's recoverable. Rank by likelihood × impact (`references/likelihood-impact.md`).

Pure doom: the chains end at the damage, not at a remedy.
